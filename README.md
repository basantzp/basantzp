## 🌐 About Me

I am a **Blockchain Enthusiast & Web3 Learner** driven by an insatiable curiosity for decentralized networks and cryptographic protocols. I believe the best way to master decentralized technology is by **learning through hands-on building**—spinning up custom testnets, writing and testing smart contracts, inspecting EVM transactions, and engineering reliable developer workflows.

- 🔭 **Currently Building & Researching**: EVM smart contract patterns, local testnet environments, decentralized APIs, and gas optimization strategies.
- 🌱 **Actively Learning**: Layer 2 rollups (Optimistic & ZK-Rollups), DeFi mechanics (AMMs, liquidity pools, flash loans), and Smart Contract Security Auditing.
- 💡 **Learning Philosophy**: Approaching every new concept with beginner's humility and an engineer's technical rigor.
- 🐧 **Environment**: Developed on Arch Linux with Hyprland and streamlined terminal tooling.
- 💬 **Let's Discuss**: Solidity, Hardhat, EVM opcodes, smart contract testing, or Linux system automation.
